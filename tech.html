Tech

We choose technology for the story, not the other way round. Our studio uses industry-standard tools for 2D, 3D and compositing, connected by one shared pipeline.

Where off-the-shelf tools fall short, we write our own scripts and tools to automate the repetitive parts of production, so artists spend their time on the work that matters.

Our stack

What every shot passes through.

(01)

2D animation

Frame-by-frame and rigged 2D, from rough to clean-up and colour.

(02)

3D

Modelling, rigging, animation, lighting and rendering.

(03)

Compositing and VFX

Keying, tracking, CGI integration and finishing.

(04)

Rendering

Render set-ups tuned for quality and turnaround.

(05)

Pipeline tools

Scripts that move files, name versions and check shots automatically.

(06)

Review
