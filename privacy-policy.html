Privacy Policy

Last updated · 19 November 2026 Read the Cookie Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Dunixab (“we”, “us”), a company based in Neyyattinkara, Trivandrum, Kerala, India, handles personal information when you visit dunixab.com or get in touch with us. We collect as little as we can, and we never sell your information.

Information you give us

When you contact us by email or phone, or send us a form on our website, such as a job application or a quote request, we receive the details you choose to share, such as:

  • your name, email address, phone number and company name;
  • the topic you’re interested in and the content of your message;
  • for job applications, your resume, current city, experience, portfolio link and any other details you send us.

Information collected automatically

We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking tools on this website. Like most websites, the servers that host it may keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request, to keep the website running and secure.

Some features rely on third-party services, which receive technical details such as your IP address when your browser connects to them:

  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare), which deliver our typefaces and 3D graphics library.

Our Cookie Policy explains these services and your cookie choices in more detail.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and continue the conversation you started.
  • To consider your application if you apply for a role with us.
  • To run, maintain and protect this website.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We don’t sell or rent your personal information, and we won’t add you to marketing emails unless you ask us to.

When we share information

We share personal information only when it’s needed to help you or required by law:

  • with the people at Dunixab best placed to handle your enquiry or application;
  • with service providers who help us run our website and communications, such as hosting and email providers, who may only use it to provide their service to us;
  • with FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), the form service that delivers the forms on our website to our inbox by email: job applications from our Open Positions page, including your resume, and quote requests from our Additional Services page. FormSubmit’s own privacy policy also applies to that data;
  • when the law, a court or a government authority requires it, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only for as long as we need them to respond and follow up, and job applications for as long as we’re recruiting for that role or similar ones. You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, but we work to keep your information protected.

Your rights

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • delete it, or withdraw consent you’ve given us;
  • address a concern or complaint about how we’ve handled it.

To make a request, email contact@dunixab.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it.

Children

This website is intended for adults. If you’re under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us on your behalf.

Other websites

Our website links to other sites, such as LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube and Google Maps. Their own privacy policies apply once you visit them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website and work change. We’ll post the new version here and update the “Last updated” date above.
